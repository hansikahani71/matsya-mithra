INLAND FISHERIES
BY MATSYA MITHRA

From 0 to Harvest - Smart Fish Farming Guide

A Community Service Project on understanding inland fish farming, pond preparation, feeding, diseases, govt schemes and profit among farmers in Mudivarthipalem.

“From 0 to Harvest - Every Pond Has a Story, Every Farmer Deserves a Guide.”
- Team Matsya Mithra, SVCN

About Our Project

This Community Service Project focuses on understanding Inland Fisheries - growing fish in ponds, tanks and lakes inside land, not in sea. In Mudivarthipalem, many farmers have ponds but they don't know correct method. They lose money due to wrong seed, wrong feed, wrong pH and diseases.

Our Website + App Matsya Mithra guides farmers step by step - Pond Preparation, Seed Release, Feeding, Disease Control, Harvest and Selling - in Telugu, Hindi and English with Voice.

Project Objectives

🐟 Understand Inland Fisheries

Understand how farmers in Mudivarthipalem do inland fish farming - pond, seed, feed, water and harvest.

🌐 Create Matsya Mithra Website / App

Build simple website + app where any farmer can learn 7 stages, schemes, calculator and chatbot in Telugu / Hindi / English.

📊 Analyse Survey

Collect and analyse survey information from farmers about their problems, losses and needs.

💡 Create Awareness

Create awareness about correct farming, PMMSY schemes, profit calculation and responsible fish farming.
